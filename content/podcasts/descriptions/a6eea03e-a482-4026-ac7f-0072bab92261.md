# Radio 1337 — Ep. 1: Replacement or Redefinition?

Released for Programmer's Day, this is the first episode of Radio 1337 (رادیو ۱۳۳۷) — the channel's podcast series. Its opening question is the one every developer is staring at right now: is artificial intelligence here to replace programmers, or to redefine what programming means?

The episode works through the noise — the flood of contradictory and often alarming AI headlines — and tries to build a coherent picture out of it. Different viewpoints on the "will AI take the programmer's job?" debate are laid side by side: where they agree, where they clash, and what they together suggest about the future of writing software.

True to the channel's style, the conversation also goes a little off-road, drifting into how software development actually unfolds inside companies — the career paths, the processes, and the realities that rarely make it into the discourse.

It closes on the note that matters most these days: one of the best things any of us can do is keep learning new skills — and keep teaching them to each other.
