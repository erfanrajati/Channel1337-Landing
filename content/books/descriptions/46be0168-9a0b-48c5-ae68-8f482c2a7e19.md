# WEF Future of Jobs Report 2025

The Future of Jobs Report 2025, published by the World Economic Forum, is a data-driven snapshot of how work is changing. Based on surveys of hundreds of the world's largest employers, it projects labor market trends through 2030.

The report maps the forces reshaping jobs: AI and information processing, robotics and automation, the energy transition, demographic shifts, and geopolitical fragmentation. It quantifies which roles are growing — data, AI, cybersecurity, and skilled trades among them — and which are shrinking, and it details the skills employers expect to need, with analytical and technological literacy at the center of a widening skills gap over the next five years.

This is not a technical book but a statistical briefing, dense with charts and macro numbers rather than code. Its value is orientation: where demand is heading before it shows up in your local job market. Read it to plan learning priorities, hiring, or career moves against real employer data.
