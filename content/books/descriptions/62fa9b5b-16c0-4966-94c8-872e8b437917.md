# Effective Modern C++

Effective Modern C++ is Scott Meyers' guide to writing good C++11 and C++14 — the same role his classic Effective C++ played for earlier versions of the language. It is organized as 42 specific guidelines, each explaining not just what to do, but why.

The guidelines cover the features that changed everyday C++ most: type deduction and auto, move semantics, perfect forwarding, and smart pointers. Later chapters tackle lambdas, the new concurrency facilities such as std::async and futures, and a final section on subtle performance issues. Every item follows the series' familiar format of clear reasoning, code examples, and pointed side notes on portability and readability.

This is the book that turns a C++98 developer into a fluent modern one. It assumes you already know the language and want to use it well. Keep it at your desk and read the items as you encounter the situations they address.
