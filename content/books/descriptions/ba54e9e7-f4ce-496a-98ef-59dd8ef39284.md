# The Little Go Book

The Little Go Book, by Karl Seguin, is a free, short introduction to the Go programming language — small enough to read in an evening, practical enough to get you writing code immediately.

The book starts with installation and tooling, then walks through the language's essentials: variables and types, arrays, slices and maps, control flow, structs, methods, and interfaces. Later chapters touch on errors, packages, and a brief introduction to concurrency with goroutines and channels. Seguin keeps the focus on how Go differs from other languages — its simplicity, explicit error handling, and lightweight concurrency model — rather than re-teaching programming from scratch.

It is a free release under a Creative Commons license and wears its brevity as a feature. Use it as your first pass over the language, then move to larger books and real projects. For developers coming from dynamic or heavyweight OO languages, it is one of the gentlest on-ramps to Go.
