# System Design Interview

System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide, Volume 1 (2nd edition) by Alex Xu, is the popular preparation book for the systems design portion of senior engineering interviews. It takes you from the basics of scaling to full interview walkthroughs, step by step.

It opens by building blocks: scaling from zero to millions of users, back-of-the-envelope estimation, and a framework for structuring any design answer. The core chapters then work through classic designs — a key-value store, consistent hashing, unique ID generation, a URL shortener, a web crawler, a notification service, a news feed, and a chat system — each presented as an interview dialogue with diagrams and alternatives weighed.

The value is the visible thought process: requirements, estimates, high-level design, deep dives, and wrap-up, in the same order an interviewer expects. Candidates get a repeatable framework plus a catalog of reusable building blocks. Even outside interviews, it is a compact tour of how real distributed systems fit together.
