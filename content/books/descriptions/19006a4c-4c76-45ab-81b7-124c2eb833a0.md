# Just for Fun

Just for Fun is the autobiography of Linus Torvalds, creator of Linux, co-written with David Diamond. This copy is a Persian translation, making the story accessible to Persian-speaking readers.

The book traces Torvalds' path from a curious kid in Helsinki tinkering with his grandfather's computer, through the famous "just a hobby, won't be big" posting that announced Linux in 1991, to the growth of a global open-source phenomenon. Alongside the personal story, Torvalds reflects on the philosophy of open source, the culture that grew around Linux, and his own views on technology, work, and what motivates people to build things together.

It reads as a candid, often funny memoir rather than a technical manual — though programmers will enjoy the details of early Linux development. The lasting insight is how a social experiment in free collaboration outgrew its humble origins. A worthwhile read for anyone curious about where open source came from.
