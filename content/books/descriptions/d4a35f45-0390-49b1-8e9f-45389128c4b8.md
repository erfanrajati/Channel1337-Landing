# System Design Interview, Volume 2

System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide, Volume 2, by Alex Xu and Sahn Lam, continues the series with a second set of complete design walkthroughs, this time leaning harder on modern data-intensive architecture. It assumes the framework from Volume 1 and applies it to fresh, harder problems.

The chapters cover proximity-based systems like finding nearby friends, mapping and navigation, a distributed message queue, metrics monitoring and alerting, ad click aggregation, hotel reservation booking with its concurrency and double-booking pitfalls, S3-style object storage, and a real-time gaming leaderboard. Each design is developed through the same structured dialogue — requirements, estimation, high-level sketch, and deep dives into data modeling and scaling.

Volume 2's designs are noticeably closer to what production teams build, with heavy attention to data flow, consistency, and storage choices. Use it to deepen the patterns from the first volume, not to replace it. Together the two books form a solid self-study course in distributed system design.
