# A Tour of C++

A Tour of C++ is Bjarne Stroustrup's compact overview of modern C++, written by the language's creator for programmers who already know how to code. Rather than teaching programming from scratch, it offers a guided walk through what makes C++ what it is: a language for building efficient, well-structured systems.

The book moves through the essentials in quick succession — types and declarations, pointers and references, containers and algorithms, classes and resource management, and templates. It devotes proper attention to C++11 features such as move semantics, lambdas, and smart pointers, and closes with chapters on concurrency and a brief history of the language's evolution.

The code-first style means you see idiomatic C++ on nearly every page, backed by Stroustrup's reasoning about why the language works the way it does. It is the fastest serious map of the territory. Read it to orient yourself, then reach for a deeper reference — including Stroustrup's own — for the details.
